SABHYASACHI

Since his debut in the Indian fashion industry, Sabyasachi has aspired to revive and give a liberating sense of esteem to Indian artisanry.
Catering to the constantly evolving and complex demography, Sabyasachi offers international twists in Indian silhouettes, colour combinations, designs and styling. He creates a strong visual statement and carves a distinct identity with his signature designs.
